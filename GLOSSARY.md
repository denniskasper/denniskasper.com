# denniskasper.com

The personal homepage of Dennis Kasper: a short introduction and a resume.

## Language

**Certification**:
A credential earned by completing a course or training, listed on the resume by its name and issuer.
_Avoid_: Certificate, course

**Certificate**:
The document an issuer hands out as proof of a Certification. A Certification has at most one Certificate.
_Avoid_: Certification, Zeugnis, diploma
